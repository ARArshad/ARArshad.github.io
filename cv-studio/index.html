Questions

Is it really free?+

Yes. Writing, editing, every template and the PDF download are free, with no watermark.

Where is my CV stored?+

In your own browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Use “Download a copy” to keep a backup or move your CV to another computer.

Will my CV get through applicant tracking systems?+

The PDF is real text, not an image, so systems can read it. For the strictest systems, choose a one column template such as Meridian, Chronicle, Harbor or Linen.

Should I include a photo?+

It depends where you apply. Photos are common in much of Europe and Asia, but usually left off in the UK, US, Canada and Australia. You can hide the photo in Design with one click.

How long should a CV be?+

One page for early career, two for most experienced people. The page counter and the CV score tell you when you are running long.
